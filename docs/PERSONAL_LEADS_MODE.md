# Personal help-request leads mode

The scheduled monitor is optimized for direct public requests from people describing their own investment/crypto/forex loss and explicitly asking what to do or requesting help.

Telegram:
- Public supergroup messages are eligible only when original, human-authored, and not forwarded.
- Broadcast channel posts are not eligible as leads. Qualifying human replies in active linked public discussions may be eligible.
- Only public post links are retained; sender IDs and usernames are not persisted.

Reddit and optional forum/RSS:
- Only direct public user-generated post/question URLs on the approved host allowlist are eligible.
- Search result pages, news, law-firm SEO pages, broker reviews, marketing pages, and unknown domains are rejected.
- Both first-person loss language and explicit help intent are required.

Google Alerts/Gmail and search-engine-based Facebook discovery are not part of the scheduled pipeline.
